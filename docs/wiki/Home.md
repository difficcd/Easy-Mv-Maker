# Easy MV Maker

A frame-by-frame animation and music-video tool, built for a tablet and a pen.

This wiki is for the explanations that are too long for the app itself. The in-app help — the **?**
button in the top bar — stays short on purpose: it lists the shortcuts, the gestures, and where
each feature lives. Anything that needs a paragraph of *why* belongs here instead.

## Where things are in the app

The app puts most of its features behind a small icon. The **?** button lists every one of them
with the icon drawn next to it, which is faster than reading about it here.

Two that catch people out:

- the **camera move** is behind the camera icon on a *cut* row, along with that cut's timing and
  its animation;
- **part animation** — move, rotate, sway, mosaic — is behind the film icon on a *layer* row.

## A thing worth knowing before you start

Cut and part animations only play while the film is playing. While you are drawing, everything sits
still. That is deliberate: a canvas that moves under the pen puts the ink somewhere other than
where you aimed.
